# Words Pocket 📚

**A simple vocabulary collection tool for English learners.**

Words Pocket helps English learners collect new vocabulary while reading books, articles, or any other English text.

Simply paste your text, and Words Pocket extracts unique words, removes duplicates, and adds only words that are not already in your pocket. This makes it easier to build and maintain a personal collection of vocabulary to review whenever you want.

## ✨ Features

- **Text Extraction:** Paste any text to extract its vocabulary.
- **Duplicate Removal:** Automatically removes repeated words from the submitted text.
- **Unique Word Collection:** Adds only words that are not already saved in your pocket.
- **No Duplicate Entries:** Previously collected words are not added again.
- **Personal Vocabulary Pocket:** Keep your collected words together for future review.

## 💡 Why Words Pocket?

When learning English through books and articles, you often encounter unfamiliar words.

Manually collecting these words can be time-consuming, especially when the same words appear multiple times.

Words Pocket simplifies this process by extracting unique words and helping you build a cleaner vocabulary collection without repeatedly saving words you already have.
